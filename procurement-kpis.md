# Procurement & Supply Chain KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase order cycle time | Time from approved requisition to PO issue | Average elapsed days |
| Supplier on-time delivery | Deliveries received by agreed date | On-time deliveries ÷ total deliveries × 100 |
| Purchase price variance | Difference between actual and reference price | (Actual − reference) × quantity |
| Inventory accuracy | Agreement between physical and recorded stock | Correctly matched records ÷ checked records × 100 |
| Stockout rate | Frequency of unavailable required items | Stockout events ÷ demand events × 100 |

Use comparable specifications, tax treatment, currency, delivery terms, and evaluation weights.
