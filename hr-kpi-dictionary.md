# HR KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Definition | Formula | Review frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turnover rate | Share of average workforce leaving in a period | Separations ÷ average headcount × 100 | Monthly |
| Absence rate | Share of scheduled work time lost to absence | Absence hours ÷ scheduled hours × 100 | Monthly |
| Time to fill | Days between approved vacancy and accepted offer | Average days for filled roles | Monthly |
| Probation pass rate | Share of reviewed employees passing probation | Passed ÷ completed reviews × 100 | Quarterly |
| Training completion | Share of assigned learning completed | Completed assignments ÷ assigned learning × 100 | Quarterly |

Define inclusion rules consistently before comparing periods or departments.
