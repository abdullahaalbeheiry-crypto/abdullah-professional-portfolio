# Facilities & Operations KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Planned maintenance compliance | Planned tasks completed on time | On-time completed tasks ÷ planned tasks × 100 |
| Reactive maintenance share | Work orders that are reactive | Reactive work orders ÷ total work orders × 100 |
| Mean time to repair | Average time to restore failed assets | Total repair time ÷ completed corrective jobs |
| Work order backlog | Open work orders at period end | Count of open work orders |
| Contractor SLA compliance | Jobs meeting agreed service levels | SLA-compliant jobs ÷ measured jobs × 100 |

Targets must be agreed against asset criticality, contracts, and baseline performance.
