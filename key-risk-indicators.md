# Key Risk Indicator Examples

| Risk area | Example KRI | Potential trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Workforce | Critical role vacancy duration | Vacancy exceeds agreed threshold |
| Facilities | Critical asset overdue maintenance | Any critical PM task overdue |
| Procurement | Single-source spend concentration | Share exceeds approved tolerance |
| Supply chain | Supplier on-time delivery | Falls below contractual target |
| Finance | Overdue receivables | Exceeds approved aging threshold |

Thresholds must be calibrated using historical data, risk appetite, and management approval.
